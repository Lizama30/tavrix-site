TAVRIX

Privacy

Tavrix is for my own use only. It reads my own Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Chat and Meet) and my own TikTok account's numbers (views, likes and followers) only to do what I ask it to. It never posts to TikTok.

It runs on my own computer. To answer, the relevant content is processed by Anthropic's Claude. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads, and Google data isn't used to train AI models, in line with Google's Limited Use rules.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google or TikTok account's settings.
